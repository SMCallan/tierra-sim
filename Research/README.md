# Research Workspace

- `research-questions.md`: canonical questions, hypotheses, and measurement decisions.
- `claim-evidence-matrix.csv`: live trace from proposed claims to required and available evidence.
- `literature-notes/`: source-level reading notes.
- `decisions/`: dated decision records for material methodological or engineering changes.

Research decisions should state the problem, alternatives, decision, rationale, consequences, and affected claims.

The accepted decision sequence is indexed in `decisions/README.md`. These records explain deliberate differences between the assessed MPR architecture and the rebuilt instrument.
